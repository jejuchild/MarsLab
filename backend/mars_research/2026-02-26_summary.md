**Date**: 2026-02-26

## Trend Analysis
Recent research in Mars rover technology has focused on enhancing autonomy, drilling capabilities, and power systems. Advancements in computer vision, machine learning, and instrument development have improved the efficiency and effectiveness of Mars rovers. The trend is expected to continue, with a growing emphasis on prolonged mission duration, subsurface exploration, and sample return.

# Mars Exploration History — Complete Mission Timeline

## Flyby Era (1960s)

| Mission | Agency | Year | Outcome |
|---------|--------|------|---------|
| Marsnik 1 & 2 | USSR | 1960 | Launch failure |
| Mars 1 | USSR | 1962 | Contact lost en route |
| **Mariner 4** | NASA | 1964 | First Mars flyby (Jul 1965); 22 images; confirmed thin CO₂ atmosphere |
| Mariner 6 & 7 | NASA | 1969 | Flyby; 201 images; confirmed CO₂ polar cap |

## Orbital Era (1971–1976)

| Mission | Agency | Year | Outcome |
|---------|--------|------|---------|
| **Mariner 9** | NASA | 1971 | First Mars orbiter; 7,329 images; discovered Olympus Mons, Valles Marineris |
| Mars 2 & 3 | USSR | 1971 | Orbiter success; Mars 3 soft-landed but failed after 20 sec |
| **Viking 1** | NASA | 1975 | First successful lander (Jul 1976); biology experiments; operated until 1982 |
| **Viking 2** | NASA | 1975 | Landed Sep 1976; seasonal frost observed; operated until 1980 |

## Gap & Revival (1988–1999)

| Mission | Agency | Year | Outcome |
|---------|--------|------|---------|
| Phobos 1 & 2 | USSR | 1988 | Mostly failed; Phobos 2 returned 37 images |
| Mars Observer | NASA | 1992 | Contact lost before orbit insertion |
| **Mars Global Surveyor** | NASA | 1996 | MOLA topography, TES mineralogy; operated until 2006 |
| **Mars Pathfinder / Sojourner** | NASA | 1996 | First rover; 550 images; 16.5 m traversed |
| Mars Climate Orbiter | NASA | 1998 | Lost (metric/imperial unit error) |
| Mars Polar Lander | NASA | 1999 | Crashed on landing |

## Modern Era (2001–2015)

| Mission | Agency | Year | Status | Key Achievement |
|---------|--------|------|--------|-----------------|
| **Mars Odyssey** | NASA | 2001 | Active | THEMIS; GRS water ice detection; longest-serving Mars orbiter |
| **Mars Express** | ESA | 2003 | Active | OMEGA mineralogy; MARSIS radar; HRSC imaging |
| **Spirit (MER-A)** | NASA | 2003 | Ended 2010 | Gusev Crater; 7.73 km; silica at Home Plate |
| **Opportunity (MER-B)** | NASA | 2003 | Ended 2019 | Meridiani; 45.16 km; jarosite; hematite blueberries |
| **MRO** | NASA | 2005 | Active | HiRISE (0.25 m/px); CRISM mineralogy; SHARAD radar |
| **Phoenix** | NASA | 2007 | Ended 2008 | 68°N; confirmed water ice; perchlorates; snow |
| **Curiosity (MSL)** | NASA | 2011 | Active | Gale Crater; ancient habitable lake; organics; Mount Sharp |
| **MAVEN** | NASA | 2013 | Contact lost Dec 2025 | Atmospheric escape; solar wind; relay |
| **MOM (Mangalyaan)** | ISRO | 2013 | Ended 2022 | India's first Mars mission; 8 years operation |

## Current Era (2016–Present)

| Mission | Agency | Year | Status | Key Achievement |
|---------|--------|------|--------|-----------------|
| **ExoMars TGO** | ESA | 2016 | Active | Trace gas mapping; CaSSIS; relay |
| **InSight** | NASA | 2018 | Ended 2022 | Seismology; core size; 1,319 marsquakes |
| **Tianwen-1 / Zhurong** | CNSA | 2020 | Orbiter active; rover dormant | Utopia Planitia; China's first Mars landing (May 2021) |
| **UAE Hope (EMM)** | UAE | 2020 | Active | Full atmospheric portrait; UV/IR |
| **Perseverance** | NASA | 2020 | Active | Jezero; sample caching; Ingenuity (72 flights); biosignature candidate (Sep 2025) |
| **ESCAPADE** | NASA | 2024 | En route | Twin orbiters; arrive Sep 2027 |
